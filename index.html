Mafia Call
Rulebook

Mafia for groups already on a video call or a live stream. Your phone holds your secret role and your ballot. A shared board narrates the game, so nobody has to moderate.

Setup

  1. 1 · HostOne person opens the site and taps Host a new game. That opens the board. Share that screen on the call, with computer sound on, so everyone hears the narrator.
  2. 2 · JoinEveryone else opens the link or room code shown on the board and signs in with a nickname. You need 4–30 players.
  3. 3 · Read your partPress and hold the card on your phone to read your secret role. Don't let anyone see your screen.

Only a few people? The host can turn on Test mode to fill the empty seats with bots. A TEST MODE banner then shows on every screen.

Night

Cameras and mics off. Every living player taps a name on their phone, every night, including night 1. The night always runs its full 45 seconds.

Why everyone taps: on a call, whoever is busy on their phone at night would give their role away. So villagers make a pick too (who they suspect), and every phone looks exactly the same. Hold Hold for your cue to read what your own pick does tonight.

  • Gangs strikeEach gang (Mafia, Werewolves, Yakuza) kills the player most of its members picked. Ties are broken at random. Picks on your own team don't count.
  • Biggest strikes firstAttacks land biggest group first. A group that was wiped out earlier that night doesn't get to strike.
  • Saves and conversionsA Doctor's protection blocks every attack. After the kills, the cult converts its pick.
  • Team chatMafia, Werewolves, Yakuza and Cultists can whisper with their own team in Notes, at night only. Nobody else can read it.

Day

  1. Dawn12s
    The board announces who died and, if the host's setting is on, their role. A Still standing count shows how many of each role are alive.
  2. Discuss2 min
    Cameras on. Accuse, defend, bluff, all on the call.
  3. Nominate30s
    Nominate someone on your phone. Anyone with 2 nominations goes on trial, up to 3 players.
  4. Vote20s
    Vote for someone on trial, or abstain. Whoever gets the most votes is out. If the top spot is tied, nobody is eliminated.
  5. Verdict25s
    Ballots are revealed one by one on the board. The eliminated player gets last words.

Dead players are ghosts. Stay silent on the call (and on stream). Ghosts don't see anyone else's role. Everything is revealed at the end.

The roles

Mafia · team

Mafia

Each night, agree with your crew on someone to kill. Whisper with them in Notes. By day, blend in.

Werewolves · rival gang

Werewolf

A separate pack with its own kill and its own chat. Werewolves don't know who the Mafia are. Outlast everyone to win.

Yakuza · rival gang

Yakuza

A third crime family that kills, plots and wins alone.

Alone

Serial Killer

Kills one player each night. Wins by being the last one standing.

Cult · team

Cultist

Each night the cult picks someone to convert; it happens silently. Killers can't be converted. The cult wins when everyone alive has joined.

Town

Doctor

Protect one player each night from any attack, yourself included, but not the same player two nights in a row.

Town

Detective

Investigate one player each night. At dawn you privately learn their side: innocent, Mafia, Werewolf, Yakuza, Cult or Serial Killer.

Town

Cult Hunter

Hunt one player each night: if they're a Cultist, they die. If the cult ever goes after you, its newest member dies instead.

Town

Cupid

On night 1, whoever you pick falls in love with you. Lovers learn each other's names, not roles. If one dies, so does the other. Lovers win together if they're the last two standing, or alongside the winning side.

Alone

Tanner

Hates life. Wins, alone, if the town votes them out, and everyone else loses. Reads as innocent to the Detective.

Town

Villager

No power. Find the killers and vote them out. At night, pick who you suspect.

Winning

  • TownEvery gang, the Serial Killer and the cult are gone.
  • A gangIt's the only killer left and it equals or outnumbers everyone else.
  • Serial KillerOnly one other player (or nobody) is left alive.
  • CultEveryone still alive is a Cultist.
  • TannerGets voted out, which ends the game at once.
  • LoversLast two alive. Surviving lovers also win alongside whichever side one of them is on.

At the end, every role is revealed along with a scene-by-scene replay of every night.

Etiquette

  • NightCameras and mics off, every time. Keep your face still.
  • DeadStay silent until the end, on the call and on stream.
  • PhonesNever show your screen. Every screen looks the same on purpose.
  • HostYou can pause, skip a phase, remove a player or start a new game at any time.
